# ADR 0105: Scope of release 0.2.0 and the step sequence S24–S29

- Status: Accepted — decided by the decision owner in chat on 2026-10-12
  ("finish to S26, then make this ready to use; users should be able to train
  a different constitutive model; this is the first version v0.2"; scope:
  isotropic and anisotropic hyperelastic models, defined by a built-in
  library or an energy formula) and on 2026-10-13 ("Update STEPS")
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Builds on: ADR 0100 (the S19+ line), the implementation plan "PI-GINOT
  S19–S26 implementation plan: top 5 features" (Claude Doc, 10 Oct 2026)

## Context

`STEPS-v2.0.md` planned S19–S26: certification (S21–S23), a declarative
problem description (S24), a cross-family encoder (S25) and a one-command
compiler (S26). Every bundle so far serves one compressible Neo-Hookean
material; the physics kernel, the plane-stress closure, the FEM solver, the
energy objective and serving all assume it, through the five functions of
`piginot.physics` and the model name `compressible_neo_hookean`. The
decision owner wants 0.2.0 to be the first version others can use, including
training a model for their own constitutive law.

The method trains without FEM labels by minimising the potential energy. It
therefore extends directly to any material with a strain-energy function
(hyperelastic), and not to path-dependent laws (plasticity, viscoelasticity),
which have no single energy to minimise and need load histories.

## Decisions

1. **Release 0.2.0 is S19–S29.** After S29 the line is feature-frozen: only
   fixes and documentation until users give feedback.
2. **Sequence.**

   | Step | Content | Depends | ADR |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | S24 | ProblemSpec, generalised exact boundary-condition lifting, FEM recipes built from the spec | S20 | 0106 |
   | S25 | Material registry: isotropic compressible hyperelastic models, energy formula, admission gate | S24 | 0107 |
   | S26 | Anisotropic hyperelastic models with in-plane fibre families | S25 | 0108 |
   | S27 | Cross-family geometry encoder (the plan's S25) | S20, S23, S24 | 0109 |
   | S28 | One-command campaign compiler and evidence report, with a material stage (the plan's S26) | S23, S24, S26 | 0110 |
   | S29 | Release 0.2.0 | S28 | 0111 |

   S27 can run in parallel with S25, S26 and S28. S29 does not wait for S27
   if S27's kill rule fires; per-family operators with certificates and
   bounds then ship.
3. **Material scope.** Compressible hyperelastic laws in 2D plane stress:
   isotropic (S25) and with in-plane fibre families (S26). A user picks a
   built-in model (Neo-Hookean, Mooney–Rivlin, Yeoh, Gent; HGO in S26) or
   writes the strain energy as a formula of the invariants and named
   parameters. Path-dependent laws are out of scope for 0.2.0.
4. **Formulas are data, never code.** A formula is parsed by a whitelist
   parser (numbers, parameters, invariants, arithmetic, `exp`, `log`,
   `sqrt`) into torch operations; `eval`, `sympify` and `parse_expr` are not
   used on it, because bundles are untrusted input. Stress and tangent come
   from automatic differentiation. Neo-Hookean keeps its closed-form path, so
   the 0.1.0 bundles stay byte-identical.
5. **Admission gate.** Every model, built-in or user-defined, must pass an
   automatic admission gate before it can be trained: stress-free reference
   state, objectivity, the declared material symmetry, consistency of stress
   and tangent with finite differences, the declared small-strain moduli, a
   unique plane-stress closure root over the strain range, ellipticity over
   the range, and optimal FEM convergence on a manufactured solution. Its
   thresholds are fixed in S25's table.
6. **Release content (S29).** A public repository with `main` at `v0.2.0`;
   PyPI packages tested on Linux, macOS and Windows; bundles and their
   sidecars distributed as hash-checked release files; hosted documentation
   with a "train your own material" tutorial run in CI; a small fast-mode
   demo; a renewed limitations sign-off.
7. **Numbering.** The steps' ADRs are 0106–0111 as in the table; the plan's
   ADR numbers 0105–0107 for S24–S26 no longer apply.

## Consequences

- `STEPS-v2.0.md` covers S19–S29. The gate tables of S24, S27 and S28 come
  from the implementation plan's S24, S25 and S26 sections; those of S25,
  S26 and S29 are written at each step's start. Each is copied into
  `STEPS-v2.0.md` with the owner's ratified thresholds before its first run.
- S24's ProblemSpec carries a material block that S25 fills with the
  registry; until then it accepts the Neo-Hookean model only.
- The deferred items of the plan stay deferred: 3D, GPU training, an agent
  layer, a hosted API or full UI, active DOE beyond S27's optional time box.
