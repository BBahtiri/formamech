# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PI-GINOT free from harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Accept responsibility for mistakes, focus
technical criticism on the work, and protect private information shared in confidence.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently exclude participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct
concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact mechanism published by the
repository host. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy to the extent
possible and will respond proportionately to the circumstances.
